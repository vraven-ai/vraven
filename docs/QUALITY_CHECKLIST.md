# Figure review checklist

Before using any figure in a paper:

1. Confirm the figure answers one scientific question.
2. Confirm there is no internal figure heading.
3. Confirm every box and arrow is connected correctly.
4. Confirm colour meaning matches the VRAVEN palette.
5. Confirm line width, area and opacity have declared meanings.
6. Confirm observational quantities are not labelled causal.
7. Confirm attention is not labelled importance without validation.
8. Confirm predictive uncertainty comes from a probability or justified estimator.
9. Confirm dashed components are described as planned or conditional.
10. Confirm schematic values are not reported as empirical results.
11. Confirm the publication export remains readable at target column width.
12. Confirm SVG and PDF render without clipped text or missing glyphs.
13. Confirm the caption identifies the diagram as architecture or methodology where appropriate.
14. Confirm the source, trace schema and rendering version are recoverable.
