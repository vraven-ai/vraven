# Changelog

## 1.0.0, 8 August 2026

Initial merge-only release of the VRAVEN DarkSide extensions.

### Added

* The ten requested dark-side analyses: decision circuitry, shortcut reliance, class-conditional internals, representation drift, failure anatomy, stability geometry, cross-method disagreement, unused capacity, adversarial rerouting and cost–evidence trade-off.
* Eight further research analyses: prediction depth, circuit multiplicity, causal synergy, activation mediation, topological shift, explanation uncertainty, mechanism transfer and high-confidence internal anomaly screening.
* A `DarkSideSession` interface for live PyTorch model audits.
* A framework-neutral `TraceDataset` for aligned dataset and checkpoint analysis.
* Standalone Plotly figures in `interactive_dark` and `publication_light` modes for all 18 result types.
* Versioned result serialisation under schema `vraven.darkside.v1`.
* A community recommendation registry for security, malware, training, model engineering, research and assurance work.
* A preflighted merge script with dry-run, conflict refusal and timestamped backup support.
* Five runnable examples and ten test modules.
* Mathematical definitions, scientific claim hierarchy, research basis, limitations, validation protocol and local merge guidance.

### Scientific boundaries

* Observational signals are not labelled causal.
* Attention, gradients, probes, representation similarity and visual separation remain observational unless a declared intervention supports a stronger claim.
* Approximate circuit discovery is not presented as a formal certificate.
* Activation patching reports recovery and reverse damage without silently treating either quantity as a pure natural indirect effect.
* Broader scope than SHAP or LIME is not presented as universal superiority.
