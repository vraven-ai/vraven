Command

vraven capture

Compile a PyTorch model into the architecture-neutral VRAVEN Mechanism IR

Minimal form

vraven capture <model>

Arguments and options

OptionMeaningDefault

--model-factory, --factoryImport path such as my_project.models:ClassifierNone

--state-dict-key—None
--strip-prefix—None
--device—cpu
--non-strict—False

--inputExample .npy, .npz or trusted tensor .pt inputNone
--output—mechanism_ir.json
