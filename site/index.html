The name and the mark

A patient observer of hidden structure.

VRAVEN means Visual Reasoning and Activation Visualisation for Explainable Networks. The raven represents observation, memory, adaptability and problem-solving: qualities that match a system built to inspect a model carefully before making a claim.

Research lineage

VRAVEN could not have taken its present form without the research that came before it.

The product translates ideas from difficult work on attribution, faithfulness, causal mechanisms and programme extraction into one usable, testable workflow. Every paper remains the work of its authors; VRAVEN's contribution is the careful synthesis, engineering and evidence discipline that connects those ideas.
