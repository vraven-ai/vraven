Identity and origin
VRAVEN

Why the raven watches the model.

Visual Reasoning and Activation Visualisation for Explainable Networks. The name describes the work: making internal neural computation inspectable, testing what matters and expressing the result as evidence people can examine.

The raven

A symbol for careful investigation.

Ravens are associated with close observation, memory, adaptability and problem-solving. That makes the raven a fitting identity for an explainability tool: VRAVEN watches the computation, follows evidence through the network, tests suspected mechanisms and remembers the limits of what was observed.

The open beak gives the mark a second meaning. An explanation should not remain hidden inside a model; it should be expressed clearly enough to inspect, question and challenge. The red wordmark is the signature. The bird itself remains natural and composed, keeping the identity focused on intelligence rather than spectacle.

From literature to product

Research ideas become usable workflows.

VRAVEN was not born from one paper. It emerged by reading across fields whose methods are powerful but often difficult to connect and apply together.

Research asks

Where is evidence?

Attribution and localisation research motivates observable evidence maps, while faithfulness work requires those maps to be tested rather than merely admired.

Research asks

What changes the decision?

Intervention and causal-abstraction research motivates controlled internal changes, explicit assumptions and bounded causal claims.

Research asks

Can the mechanism be recovered?

Mechanistic interpretability and programme-extraction research motivates circuits, executable approximations and measured fidelity.

Product answers

One traceable workflow

VRAVEN connects capture, explanation, intervention, comparison and reporting while keeping observational, interventional and formal evidence distinct.

Acknowledgement

VRAVEN would not exist in its present form without the papers in the research foundations. Their authors created the underlying ideas, methods and critical tests. VRAVEN's role is to study that work, translate compatible innovations into software and make the resulting evidence easier to run, inspect and communicate. Citation is therefore part of the product's identity, not an afterthought.
